# IRHIS app – user manual

Step-by-step guide to recording a movement analysis session with the IRHIS app and five Movella DOT sensors.

---

## Before you start

Check that:

- The phone's **Bluetooth is on**.
- The 5 Movella DOT sensors are **charged** and switched on (press each sensor's button until the light comes on).
- The phone is connected to the internet (Wi-Fi or mobile data).

**Tip:** put a numbered sticker (1 to 5) on each sensor. It prevents mix-ups when assigning positions in the app.

---

## 1. Sign in

1. Open the **IRHIS** app.
2. In **Access code**, enter the code you were given.
3. In **Password**, enter your password.
4. Tap **Sign In**.

> To check what you typed in the password field, tap the eye icon on the right of the field.

The app does not keep you signed in after it is closed, so you will sign in each time you open it.

---

## 2. Connect the sensors

1. Tap the **Sensors** tab at the bottom of the screen.
2. Tap **Search Sensors**. While the app searches, the button shows **Scanning...** (for about 10 seconds).
3. As sensors appear in the list, tap **Connect** on each one.
4. A connected sensor gets a green border and shows **Connected** and its battery level.
5. Repeat until **all 5 sensors are connected**.

> If a sensor does not appear, tap **Search Sensors** again. If it still does not appear, tap **Restart Search** (this disconnects all sensors and starts again).

---

## 3. Assign the positions

Under each connected sensor, tap the position it will be worn on:

| Position in the app | Where on the body |
|---|---|
| Right knee | Outer side of the right leg, just above the knee, facing out |
| Right ankle | Outer side of the right leg, just above the ankle, facing out |
| Left knee | Outer side of the left leg, just above the knee, facing out |
| Left ankle | Outer side of the left leg, just above the ankle, facing out |
| Lower back | Middle of the lower back, just above the buttocks |

**Right and left are always the patient's right and left**, not yours when you face the patient.

The card at the top of the Sensors tab shows how many of the 5 positions are covered and which are missing. When all five are covered it shows **Sensors ready** with a green tick.

---

## 4. Put the sensors on the patient

1. Place each sensor on its position using the elastic straps. The leg sensors go on the **outer side** of the leg, facing out, lined up along the leg.
2. Tighten the strap firmly but comfortably. The sensor must not slide or turn during the movement.
3. Make sure the sensor sits flat on the skin or on tight clothing.
4. Check that each sensor's number matches the position you assigned in the app.

---

## 5. Choose the exercise

1. Tap the **Live Session** tab.
2. Clinicians: tap **Select Patient** and choose the patient.
3. Tap **Select Exercise** and choose the exercise to perform.
4. Tap **BLE Stream**. The **Record Exercise** screen opens.

> Choosing the patient (clinicians) and the exercise is required. Without them the app will not open the recording screen.

At the top of the Record Exercise screen, check that the sensor card says **Sensors ready**. If it does not, tap **Manage Sensors** to fix the connections or positions, then go back.

---

## 6. Record the exercise

1. Ask the patient to stand upright in the starting position and **stay completely still for about 2 seconds**. The app uses these first seconds as the reference.
2. Tap **Start Exercise**. The app confirms that it is recording from all sensors.
3. The patient performs the exercise.
4. When finished, tap **Stop Exercise**.

> While recording, the screen shows **Recording exercise data...** and the number of packets received from each sensor. Sensor connections cannot be changed during a recording.

---

## 7. Analyse and save

1. Tap **Analyze Exercise**.
2. Wait while the app shows **Analyzing...**.
3. When **Analysis Complete** appears, the results are saved to the patient's record.

---

## Common problems

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| A sensor does not appear when scanning | Check it is switched on and charged. Tap **Search Sensors** again, or **Restart Search**. |
| **Start Exercise** is greyed out | Not all 5 positions are covered. Tap **Manage Sensors** and check that all 5 sensors are connected and have a position. |
| **No Data Received** appears | One or more sensors lost the connection. Stop, reconnect the sensors in the Sensors tab and record again. |
| A sensor disconnects during the exercise | Stop the exercise, reconnect the sensor and record again. |
| The patient moved at the very start | Record again. Movement in the first 2 seconds affects all results. |
| The app does not continue after signing in | Check the internet connection. |

---

## Quick summary

**Sign in → Sensors tab: connect the 5 sensors and assign the 5 positions → put the sensors on the patient → Live Session: select patient and exercise → BLE Stream → stand still 2 s → Start Exercise → Stop Exercise → Analyze Exercise**
